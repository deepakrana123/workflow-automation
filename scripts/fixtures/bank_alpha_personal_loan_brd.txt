BANK ALPHA PERSONAL LOAN PROGRAM
================================

Eligibility Criteria & Terms
Version 1.0 | Effective Date: 2026-01-01

---

1. APPLICANT DEMOGRAPHICS

The Personal Loan Program is open to Indian residents meeting the following age criteria:
- Applicant age must be between 21 and 65 years (inclusive)
- Self-employed and salaried professionals are eligible
- Joint applications with valid co-applicants are accepted

---

2. CREDIT ASSESSMENT

All applicants undergo a comprehensive credit evaluation based on credit history:
- Minimum CIBIL credit score required: 650
- Credit score evaluation is mandatory for all loan amounts
- Applicants with scores below 650 may reapply after 6 months of credit history improvement

---

3. INCOME VERIFICATION

Applicants must demonstrate stable and verifiable income:
- Monthly salary must exceed ₹25,000 (twenty-five thousand rupees)
- Income verification through payroll records, ITR, or bank statements required
- Minimum 2 years of consistent income history required

---

4. LOAN AMOUNT LIMITS

Loan amounts are constrained by the following policy:
- Minimum loan amount: ₹50,000 (fifty thousand rupees)
- Maximum loan amount: ₹25,00,000 (twenty-five lakhs rupees)
- Loan amounts beyond ₹10 lakhs require additional collateral documentation

---

5. APPLICABLE INTEREST RATES

Interest rates are determined based on credit profile and market conditions:
- Applicable interest rates range from 9.5% to 15% per annum
- Rates are set per RBI guidelines and Bank Alpha's internal policy mandate
- Lower rates apply to higher credit scores
- Rate adjustments follow quarterly market reviews

---

6. EMPLOYMENT DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

Salaried applicants must provide comprehensive employment history:
- Minimum 2 years of employment history is mandatory
- Required documents: Salary slips (last 12 months), Offer letter, Form 16
- Self-employed applicants require additional business registration and ITR documents
- Employment verification may be conducted directly with employer

---

7. SPECIAL PROGRAMS

Premium programs are available for select categories:
- Employees of partner organizations are eligible for pre-approved loans up to ₹10 lakhs
- Pre-approved loans follow streamlined approval process per corporate tie-up agreement
- Corporate lending policy mandates expedited document verification
- Partner company employees may receive preferential interest rates

---

8. COLLATERAL REQUIREMENTS

Personal loans may be structured with optional collateral backing:
- Acceptable collateral includes: fixed deposits, securities, or property
- Collateral pledging is optional for loans below ₹5 lakhs
- Collateral pledging is mandatory for loans exceeding ₹10 lakhs
- Collateral valuation and insurance requirements apply per standard norms

---

POLICY STATEMENT

This Personal Loan Program is governed by Bank Alpha's lending mandate, RBI regulations,
and internal credit policies. All applicants must comply with Know Your Customer (KYC)
requirements and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) guidelines. The bank reserves the right to
decline applications based on credit assessment outcomes.
