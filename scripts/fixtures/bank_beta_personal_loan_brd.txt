BANK BETA ADAPTIVE PERSONAL LOAN SOLUTION
==========================================

Eligibility Matrix & Product Terms
Version 2.0 | Effective Date: 2026-02-01

---

1. APPLICANT AGE & RESIDENCY

The Adaptive Personal Loan Solution is available to eligible residents with the following criteria:
- Applicant age must be between 25 and 60 years (inclusive)
- Valid Indian residency and permanent address required
- Age verification through government-issued ID mandatory
- Joint applications with spouse or family members allowed with co-applicant income

---

2. CREDIT PROFILE ASSESSMENT

Bank Beta employs enhanced credit evaluation standards:
- Minimum CIBIL credit score requirement: 700
- Credit score of 700+ qualifies for standard rates and full loan amount
- Credit scores between 650-699 may qualify with higher interest rates
- Credit scores below 650 subject to additional collateral requirements

---

3. INCOME REQUIREMENTS

Applicants must demonstrate strong income stability:
- Annual income must be at least ₹3,00,000 (three lakhs rupees)
- Monthly equivalent: Minimum ₹25,000 per month
- Income documentation: Last 2 years ITR, recent bank statements, payroll records
- Self-employed applicants require audited financial statements

---

4. LOAN PRODUCT LIMITS

Adaptive loan structuring with flexible limits:
- Minimum loan amount: ₹1,00,000 (one lakh rupees)
- Maximum loan amount: ₹50,00,000 (fifty lakhs rupees)
- Loan amounts scaled based on income, credit score, and existing liabilities
- Tenure flexibility available from 12 to 84 months

---

5. EMI-TO-INCOME RATIO CONSTRAINT

Bank Beta enforces strict debt-to-income limitations:
- EMI amount must not exceed 40% of monthly salary
- Total existing EMI plus new EMI should not exceed 50% of net monthly income
- Liability assessment conducted per RBI debt-to-income standards
- Floating rate products subject to additional margin buffer

---

6. PROCESSING FEE STRUCTURE

Transparent fee framework based on credit profile:
- Processing fee ranges from 0.5% to 1.5% of loan amount
- Fees charged upfront and debited from disbursement amount
- Fee structure: 0.5% for credit score 750+, 1.0% for 700-749, 1.5% for below 700
- Loan mandate guidelines govern fee calculation and applicability

---

7. JOINT APPLICATIONS & CO-APPLICANTS

Enhanced eligibility through joint application structure:
- Co-applicants with independent income improve eligibility significantly
- Joint applications require co-applicant's full documentation and credit evaluation
- Combined income and co-applicant's income considered for loan eligibility
- Co-applicant must meet minimum age requirement of 25 years

---

8. LOAN TENURE OPTIONS

Flexible repayment periods accommodate diverse financial planning:
- Available tenure periods: 12, 24, 36, 48, 60, 72, 84 months
- Monthly installment calculated using reducing balance method
- Prepayment allowed without penalty after 12-month lock-in period
- Tenure selection impacts interest rate and total repayment amount

---

9. EXISTING LIABILITIES ASSESSMENT

Comprehensive evaluation of current financial obligations:
- Existing loan EMI amounts must be verified from credit bureau
- Total debt-to-income ratio calculated per RBI prescriptive guidelines
- Applicants with high existing liabilities may qualify for reduced loan amounts
- Refinancing of existing loans may be considered in specific circumstances

---

10. DOCUMENTATION & VERIFICATION

Complete documentation required for all applicants:
- KYC documents: Aadhaar, PAN, Passport or Voter ID
- Address proof: Utility bill, bank statement, or rental agreement
- Income proof: Last 2 years ITR, recent bank statements, salary slips
- Employment verification conducted with employer or business registration authority

---

CORPORATE PROGRAMS

Bank Beta offers specialized programs for corporate sector:
- Salaried employees of registered corporations may access expedited approval
- Corporate tie-up agreements enable pre-approved loan products
- Employee referral programs with reduced processing timelines
- Group discount rates available for large corporate employee bases

---

POLICY STATEMENT

The Adaptive Personal Loan Solution adheres to Bank Beta's lending policies,
RBI Prudential Framework guidelines, and applicable financial regulations. All applicants
must comply with Extended KYC and AML verification requirements. The bank reserves
discretionary authority to approve or decline applications based on credit assessment.
