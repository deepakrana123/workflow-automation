1. PURPOSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This document defines the end-to-end workflow for processing a car loan application from submission to vehicle delivery confirmation, including dealer coordination, RC hypothecation, and insurance verification.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. WORKFLOW DESCRIPTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 Trigger Event

The workflow begins when a customer submits a car loan application. The application captures the vehicle details (make, model, variant), dealer name, on-road price, own contribution amount, and employment and income details.

2.2 KYC and Income Verification

The system initiates KYC verification for the applicant. The system sends the document checklist to the customer listing required documents: driving licence, PAN, Aadhaar, address proof, income documents, and bank statements.

Income documents are verified by the credit team. For salaried employees, the last 3 months' salary slips and latest Form 16 are required.

2.3 Credit Bureau Check

A CIBIL bureau check is run for the applicant. The minimum acceptable CIBIL score for car loans is 680.

Business Rules:
- Score below 680: Send low CIBIL alert to credit team, reject application, send rejection letter to customer
- Score 680 and above: Proceed to vehicle valuation

2.4 Vehicle Valuation

The system initiates vehicle valuation for used car loans. For new car loans, the vehicle valuation is based on the on-road price from the dealer invoice.

For used cars, an empanelled valuer physically inspects the vehicle and submits a valuation report.

2.5 Dealer Invoice Verification

The system verifies the proforma invoice submitted by the authorized dealer. The invoice must match the vehicle details in the application. Any discrepancy is raised as a query back to the dealer.

The bank coordinates with the dealer to confirm vehicle availability and delivery timeline.

2.6 Insurance Verification

Before disbursement, the customer must submit a comprehensive motor insurance policy. The system verifies that:
- The insurance covers the full on-road value of the vehicle
- The bank is noted as the financier (hypothecation clause)
- The policy is valid for at least 1 year

Business Rules:
- Disbursement must not proceed without verified insurance
- Insurance must name the bank as the hypothecation holder

2.7 Credit Assessment and Sanction

Based on CIBIL score, income verification, and vehicle details, the underwriting team approves the loan. The approved loan amount must not exceed 90% of the on-road price (10% minimum own contribution).

The system issues the sanction letter to the customer with the approved loan amount, interest rate, tenure, and EMI details.

2.8 EMI Calculation and Repayment Schedule

The system calculates the EMI and generates a full repayment schedule for the approved tenure. The repayment schedule is sent to the customer.

2.9 Disbursement to Dealer

The loan amount is disbursed directly to the dealer's bank account (not to the customer). The disbursement is made against the dealer invoice. The disbursement advice is sent to both the customer and the dealer.

2.10 Vehicle Delivery Confirmation

Once the dealer receives the funds, the vehicle is delivered to the customer. The system records the delivery confirmation once the customer acknowledges receipt of the vehicle.

2.11 RC Hypothecation

After vehicle delivery, the customer must get the RC (Registration Certificate) endorsed with the bank's name as the hypothecation holder at the Regional Transport Office. The bank sends an RC endorsement notice to the customer with instructions and deadline.

Business Rules:
- RC with hypothecation must be submitted to the bank within 30 days of delivery
- Failure to submit results in follow-up notices

2.12 Post-Disbursement Documents

The bank collects post-disbursement documents including the RC with hypothecation endorsement, insurance policy copy, and vehicle delivery receipt.

3. BUSINESS RULES SUMMARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- Minimum CIBIL score: 680
- Maximum LTV: 90% of on-road price
- Insurance mandatory before disbursement
- Disbursement to dealer account only
- RC hypothecation within 30 days

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
4. ACTORS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- Customer: Applicant and vehicle owner
- Authorized Dealer: Vehicle seller, invoice provider
- Credit Team: Income and CIBIL review
- Vehicle Finance Operations: Disbursement and post-disbursement
- Insurance Verifier: Policy validation
- RTO: RC endorsement authority
