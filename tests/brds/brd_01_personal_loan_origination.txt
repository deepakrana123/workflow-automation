1. PURPOSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This document defines the end-to-end business workflow for processing a personal loan application from submission to disbursement. The workflow applies to all retail customers applying for personal loans up to INR 25,00,000.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. SCOPE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

In scope:
- New personal loan applications submitted via branch or digital channel
- Credit assessment and underwriting
- Documentation verification
- Loan sanction and disbursement

Out of scope:
- Top-up loans on existing accounts
- Business loans
- Secured loans

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. WORKFLOW DESCRIPTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1 Trigger Event

The workflow is initiated when a customer submits a personal loan application through any channel (branch, mobile app, or website). The application must contain the applicant's personal details, employment information, and loan amount requested.

3.2 KYC Verification

Upon receiving the application, the system must initiate KYC verification for the applicant. The KYC team collects and verifies identity documents (Aadhaar, PAN), address proof, and income documents.

If KYC documents are incomplete, the system sends a document checklist to the customer via SMS and email with a 7-day deadline. If the customer does not respond within 7 days, the application is marked as abandoned.

Once all documents are received, the system verifies the submitted documents for authenticity and completeness.

3.3 Credit Bureau Check

After successful KYC verification, the system runs a CIBIL bureau check to retrieve the applicant's credit score and credit history report. The bureau check must be completed within 2 minutes of initiation.

Business Rules:
- If CIBIL score is below 650, the application must be rejected. The system sends a low CIBIL alert to the credit risk team and a rejection letter is sent to the customer.
- If CIBIL score is 650 or above, the workflow proceeds to income verification.

3.4 Income Verification

The credit team verifies the applicant's income through salary slips, bank statements, and ITR documents. For salaried employees, the last 3 months' salary slips are mandatory. For self-employed applicants, 2 years of ITR is required.

Business Rules:
- Debt-to-income ratio must not exceed 50%.
- Minimum monthly income must be INR 25,000 for loan amounts above INR 5,00,000.

3.5 Credit Assessment and Underwriting

Based on the CIBIL score, income, and employment stability, the underwriting team performs a credit assessment. The system calculates the risk score for the applicant.

Business Rules:
- Applications with risk score above 70 are auto-approved.
- Applications with risk score between 50 and 70 require manual underwriting review.
- Applications with risk score below 50 are rejected.

For approved applications, the underwriting team approves the underwriting decision.

3.6 Loan Sanction

Upon underwriting approval, the system generates a loan sanction letter specifying the approved loan amount, interest rate, tenure, and EMI. The sanction letter is sent to the customer for acceptance.

The system also sends the loan offer to the customer via email and SMS.

3.7 Loan Agreement and EMI Calculation

After the customer accepts the offer, the system generates a formal loan agreement document. The system calculates the EMI based on the principal amount, interest rate, and tenure. A full repayment schedule is generated and sent to the customer.

3.8 Disbursement

Once the loan agreement is signed and returned, the system disburses the loan amount directly to the customer's savings account. A disbursement advice is sent to the customer confirming the transfer.

3.9 Post-Disbursement

After disbursement, the system links the insurance policy to the loan account as per regulatory requirement. The system sends payment reminders 5 days before each EMI due date.

4. ACTORS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- Customer: Submits application, provides documents, accepts offer
- Credit Risk Team: Reviews CIBIL alerts, performs manual underwriting
- KYC Team: Verifies documents
- Loan Operations: Processes disbursement
- Relationship Manager: Assists customer through process

5. EXTERNAL SYSTEMS

- CIBIL/Bureau API: Credit score retrieval
- Core Banking System (CBS): Account lookup and fund transfer
- Document Management System (DMS): Document storage
- SMS Gateway: Customer notifications
- Email Service: Document delivery
