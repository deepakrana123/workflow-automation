1. PURPOSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This document defines the complete home loan origination workflow from application receipt to final disbursement. It covers credit assessment, property appraisal, legal verification, and tranche-based disbursement for under-construction properties.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. WORKFLOW DESCRIPTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1 Trigger Event

The workflow begins when a customer submits a home loan application. The application captures property details, loan amount requested, employment details, co-applicant information (if any), and proof of own contribution.

2.2 KYC and Document Collection

The system initiates KYC verification for the primary applicant and all co-applicants. A document checklist is sent to the customer specifying required documents: identity proof, address proof, income documents, property documents, and bank statements for the last 6 months.

2.3 Credit Bureau Check

A CIBIL bureau check is run for all applicants. The system runs this for the primary applicant and co-applicant simultaneously.

Business Rules:
- Minimum CIBIL score of 700 required for home loans
- If score is below 700, send CIBIL low alert to credit team and reject the application
- Co-applicant CIBIL must be above 650

2.4 Income Verification

The credit team verifies income documents. For salaried applicants, the last 6 months' salary slips and Form 16 are verified. For self-employed applicants, ITR for 3 years and CA-certified financials are required.

The loan-to-income ratio must not exceed 5 times the annual income.

2.5 Property Valuation

Once income verification is complete, the system initiates property valuation. An empanelled valuer is assigned to conduct a physical site visit. The technical visit is scheduled within 3 working days.

After the site visit, the technical report is generated by the valuer and submitted to the bank's underwriting team.

2.6 Legal Verification

Simultaneously with property valuation, the system initiates legal verification of the property title. Property documents are sent to the bank's empanelled legal team for title search and encumbrance certificate verification.

If the legal team raises a query on the property documents, a legal query notification is sent to the customer to provide clarification or additional documents.

Upon successful legal verification, the legal team marks the title as clear.

Business Rules:
- Property must be legally clear with no encumbrances
- Title must be in the seller's name with no disputes
- All approvals (building plan, occupancy certificate) must be in order

2.7 Underwriting Approval

Based on credit assessment, property valuation, and legal clearance, the underwriting team reviews and approves the underwriting decision. The LTV (Loan-to-Value) ratio must not exceed 80%.

2.8 Sanction Letter

Upon underwriting approval, the system issues a formal sanction letter to the customer. The sanction letter specifies the approved loan amount, interest rate (fixed or floating), tenure, and EMI schedule.

2.9 Loan Agreement and Mortgage Registration

After the customer accepts the sanction, a loan agreement is generated. The mortgage is registered with the sub-registrar office as an equitable mortgage.

The bank collects original property documents from the customer before disbursement.

2.10 Insurance Linkage

Before disbursement, home loan insurance (property and life insurance) must be linked to the loan account. The insurance reminder is sent to the customer if insurance documents are pending.

2.11 Disbursement

For ready-to-move properties, the full loan amount is disbursed as a single tranche directly to the seller's account. For under-construction properties, the loan is disbursed in tranches linked to construction milestones.

Each tranche disbursement triggers a notification to the developer. The disbursement advice is sent to the customer after each tranche.

2.12 Post-Disbursement Documentation

After disbursement, the bank collects post-disbursement documents including the registered sale deed and updated property card.
